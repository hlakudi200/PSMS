## Summary
Weekly timetable grid showing the teacher's own periods. (US-TCH-013)

## Scope
- `app/teacher/schedule/page.tsx`.
- Filter TimetableSlots by `teacherId = current user`.
- Tabs for daily / weekly / term view.
- Range filter.
- Show total teaching periods per week (workload indicator).

## Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] Grid renders periods correctly (subject / grade / room / time).
- [ ] Free periods visible.
- [ ] Workload count visible.
- [ ] PDF / iCal export deferred to a follow-up ticket -- not required here.

## Business Rules
SBR-TT-001 (structure), TT-005 (workload limits -- max 35 periods/week SA labor law).
