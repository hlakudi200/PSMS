## Summary
Teacher's online lessons page + schedule modal. (US-TCH-004)

## Scope
- `app/teacher/lessons/page.tsx` + `ScheduleLessonModal`.
- Form (Zod): subject / class, scheduled date+time (>= 24 h ahead), duration 30-180 min, title, description, attachments.
- Conflict pre-check by calling `getByClassSubjectAsync` for the chosen class and showing overlap warnings.
- Validate school hours 07:00-17:00 SA.
